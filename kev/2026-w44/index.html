Known exploited vulnerabilities: Oct 26 – Nov 1, 2026

10 new CISA KEV entries · EPSS as of 2026-11-02

CISA added 10 vulnerabilities to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog between Oct 26 – Nov 1, 2026: each one is confirmed to be exploited in the wild. 0 are linked to ransomware campaigns, 3 have a public exploit, and 6 have an EPSS score of 10% or more. The catalog now holds 1766 entries.

CVE-2026-46300 — Linux Kernel Missing Initialization of a Variable Vulnerability
